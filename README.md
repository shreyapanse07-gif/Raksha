# Raksha
Raksha is a GIS-based decision-support platform that helps disaster-management authorities decide which vulnerable Uttarakhand villages to relocate first. It combines terrain slope and rainfall into a hazard map that rates all 15,201 villages from Low to Severe, and that map was checked against real disasters (Kedarnath, Joshimath, Chamoli) and against known-safe towns. For the 4,470 villages in High or Severe zones, it then calculates a 0-100 priority score from vulnerable population, distance to a suitable relocation site, and healthcare access. That score sorts them into Immediate, Short-Term, and Medium-Term relocation tiers, and it also scores candidate relocation sites for road, hospital, and school access. Finally, the villages in the Immediate tier are checked against a live rainfall forecast to see whether conditions are turning dangerous. The result is an interactive dashboard that answers where the danger is, which villages to act on first, where to move people, and whether the weather is close to a trigger point.
